The LCP element is the hero's intro paragraph, not the `<h1>` — both sit behind the same Framer
Motion `opacity: 0` entrance, which is what task 2.3 addresses. A Lighthouse SEO score of 100 is
worth reading correctly: it means the one page is technically well formed. It says nothing about
whether the site has pages worth ranking, which is what the rest of the plan is about.

Failing audits worth keeping in view:

- `unused-javascript` — about 24 KB estimated saving.
- `render-blocking-insight`, `network-dependency-tree-insight`.
- `max-potential-fid` — 160 ms.
- `color-contrast` and `label-content-name-mismatch` — accessibility, not SEO, but both are real and
  neither is in the SEO plan. Worth their own branch.
